Rodney Zadeh

Operator. Builder. Writer.

Technology / Systems / Axioms

The world is changing beneath us, and there's no stopping it. That part isn't in question. What's still open is what we put into it while it's being built, and who ends up carrying the cost of those choices.

I'm in the middle of it like everyone else. I read. I trace claims back to where they came from. I write the questions down as precisely as I can.

I don't claim expertise yet. I'm early, and I'm doing the work to get there. What I do have is an eye for the moment a system stops doing what it says it does, and a commitment to becoming a builder in this space. Because you can't argue a system into behaving differently. At some point, you have to build a better one.

No system becomes trustworthy by accident. Someone has to decide to put that into the architecture, or nobody does.

And right now, the people making those decisions are moving fast, with very little scrutiny.

That's the part that fascinates me.
